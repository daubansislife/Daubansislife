हमारे कुल देवताOur Kul Devta

श्री शिव कतरमल स्वामी

Shri Shiv Katarmal Swami

हमारे कुल देवता का मन्दिर गाँव में रहने वाले और जहाँ भी बसे हों, हर ग्रामवासी की रक्षा करता है। आप चाहे जितनी दूर हों, आशीर्वाद आपके साथ रहता है।

The temple of our family deity watches over every villager, in the village and wherever they have settled. However far you are, the blessing stays with you. Come and bow at home.

प्रिय ग्रामवासियो Dear villagers

आपके बच्चे शहर में बड़े हो रहे हैं, पर उनकी जड़ें दौबांस में हैं। जो बच्चा अपने गाँव, अपने कुल देवता और अपने लोगों को जानता है, वह कभी अपनी पहचान नहीं भूलता। साल में कम से कम एक बार, किसी त्योहार या छुट्टी में, उन्हें दौबांस ज़रूर लाइए। उन्हें खेत दिखाइए, मंदिर ले जाइए, दादा-दादी और पूरे गाँव से मिलवाइए। गाँव आपकी राह देख रहा है। आइए, घर लौटिए।

Your children are growing up in the city, but their roots are in Dobans. A child who knows their village, their Kul Devta and their people never forgets who they are. Bring them to Dobans at least once a year, for a festival or a holiday. Show them the fields, take them to the temple, and let them meet their grandparents and the whole village. The village is waiting for you. Come home.

घर फर्किने बाटोFrom visitor to villager

  1. Visit for a festivalCome for the next village celebration and meet the community again.
  2. Stay for a seasonSpend a month during harvest or summer. Work remotely if you can.
  3. Renovate or buildRepair the family house or start a small project, such as a homestay or farm.
  4. SettleMake Dobans your base, fully or part of the year.

हाम्रो पहलOur village initiative

Dobans is organising to bring its people home. To turn this into reality we are asking local government and administration to support us with:

  • Road and transport access to the village
  • Reliable water, electricity and internet
  • Support for homestays, farming and local enterprise
  • Recognition of Dobans in development and tourism plans
